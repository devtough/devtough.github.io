How to Opt In

Evan's Assistant is a private text-message assistant run by Evan K., a sole proprietor, for friends and family only. It is not open to the public, and there is no web sign-up form. Everyone who receives messages completes both steps below. No one is messaged until both steps are complete.

Step 1: Verbal consent

Evan K. personally asks each friend or family member, in person or by phone, using this script:

“I’d like to add you to Evan’s Assistant, my personal assistant text line. You’ll get conversational text messages like reminders and confirmations when you ask it for help. Message frequency varies, typically a few per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP at any time to opt out. The terms are at devtough.github.io/terms.html and the privacy policy is at devtough.github.io/privacy.html. Is that okay?”

Only people who say yes continue to step 2. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Step 2: Text START to confirm

The person then texts START to the assistant’s number, which Evan K. gives them directly. The assistant replies with a confirmation:

START
Evan’s Assistant: You’re enrolled in Evan’s Assistant, a private assistant run by Evan K. for friends and family. Msg frequency varies, typically a few per day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Help and opt-out

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Msg&Data Rates May Apply.

You have successfully been unsubscribed. You will not receive any more messages from this number. Reply START to resubscribe.
