Program description

Evan's Assistant is a private personal assistant available only to Evan K.'s family members and personal friends. After opting in, participants text the assistant to request help with everyday personal tasks, and the assistant replies with the requested information or a confirmation. Messages may include reminders, schedule updates, shopping lists, and confirmations of completed errands. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

How to opt in

Participants first give verbal consent directly to Evan K., then confirm enrollment by texting START to the assistant's number, which Evan K. provides directly. You will receive a confirmation message. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your requests, typically a few messages per day.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your plan.

How to opt out

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. To rejoin, text START. UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, and QUIT also work.
