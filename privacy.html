Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Evan's Assistant, operated by Evan K., a sole proprietor, handles information for its text messaging program.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the assistant: your name, your mobile phone number, the date you gave consent, and the content of messages you exchange with the assistant.

How we use it

Your information is used only to respond to your requests, send the reminders and confirmations you ask for, and keep a record of your consent and opt-out choices.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers that operate the messaging service (such as our SMS provider) solely to deliver messages, and may be disclosed if required by law. We never sell, rent, or trade your information.

Retention and security

Information is kept only as long as needed to operate the assistant and meet consent record-keeping obligations, and is protected with reasonable safeguards.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message at any time to opt out. To ask about or delete your information, email brews.sachem-6o@icloud.com.
