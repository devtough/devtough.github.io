Evan's Assistant

Evan's Assistant is a private, personal text-message assistant operated by Evan K. for friends and family. It is not a commercial service and is not open to the public.

Participants text the assistant to request help with everyday personal tasks, such as reminders, schedule updates, shopping lists, and confirmations of errands. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.
